You picked your idea. Now what?

Most people stall right here — they know what they want to build but have no clue what to do first. So they open a blank doc, stare at it, and go back to scrolling.

The One-Page Launch Plan compresses your entire launch strategy onto a single page. In about 15 minutes, you'll have a clear, actionable plan you can start executing today.

What you'll fill in:

- The Big Idea — What you're building, in one sentence

- Who It's For — Your target audience, described specifically

- The Core Offer — What they get and why they'd pay for it

- MVP Scope — The absolute minimum you need to ship a v1

- 3 First Steps — The next three concrete actions to take this week

- Launch Date — A real date you're committing to

- Success Metric — How you'll know if it's working
