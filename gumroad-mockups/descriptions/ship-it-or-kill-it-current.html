A 32-page playbook that gives side-project shippers a phased framework for going from stalled idea to either a live product or a confident decision to walk away — in 90 days.

The core structure:

Three 4-week phases, each ending with a Ship or Kill checkpoint so you're never more than a month away from a real decision.

Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4): Foundation & First Ship — The Napkin Test (distill your idea to one person, one problem, one success metric), build an ugly prototype, get it in front of 5 real people, and hit your first checkpoint.

Phase 2 (Weeks 5-8): Feedback & Iteration — Expand to 20+ users, track what they actually use vs. where they drop off, fix the highest-impact thing, and hit checkpoint two.

Phase 3 (Weeks 9-12): The Decision — Stress-test whether it can grow without you, whether anyone will pay, and whether you actually want to keep working on it. Then commit or close. No "keep tinkering indefinitely."

The tools inside:

  • A weekly sprint template designed for people with full-time jobs (5-10 hours/week)
  • A daily standup format (3 questions, 60 seconds)
  • The 7 Traps That Kill Side Projects (architecture astronaut, research spiral, feature creep, perfectionism paralysis, solo hero, wrong metrics, "one more thing" delay)
  • A 5-dimension scoring framework for the final Ship or Kill decision (user traction, problem-solution fit, revenue signal, energy, market timing) with a 5-15 point scale and clear verdicts
  • A printable 12-week tracker
  • Mindset reframes for the four beliefs that keep people stuck

The philosophy running through it: Momentum beats perfection. Ship ugly. Let the market tell you what matters. Killing a project with data isn't failure — it's the most senior move you can make.
